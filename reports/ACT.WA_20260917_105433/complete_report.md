# Bull Researcher

## Round 1

Look, I get the pullback from 46.40 — but calling this a broken stock ignores the bigger picture. ACT is still **26% above a rising 200-day SMA** at 34.93. That's not a topping pattern; it's a healthy consolidation inside a year-long rally from 27 PLN.

The fundamentals back this up. Q1 2026 revenue hit 728M PLN (+9.8% YoY), EPS climbed to 3.59 PLN, and ROE expanded from 9.65% to 12.79% over four quarters. At 13× P/E with a PEG of 0.57, you're not paying growth premiums — you're getting growth at a discount.

And here's the kicker: **management is buying back shares**. Two buyback disclosures this week alone, plus capital reduction via treasury share cancellation. That's 16.5M shares down to 14.1M — real float shrinkage, real EPS accretion. When insiders are buying at ~44 PLN, why would you sell at 43.95?

The Sep 4 washout to 42.75 on 38,558 shares was the capitulation event. Price recovered, held above 43, and the buyback program provides a structural bid. RSI at 47 isn't oversold, but it's not overbought either — there's room to run back to 45.15 and 46.40 without stretching valuations.

Yes, MACD turned negative and we're below the 50-day SMA. But beta is 0.255 — this isn't a momentum rocket that needs to be above every moving average. It's a low-volatility compounder having a 5% dip. The bear case requires a break below 42.75, and we haven't even tested it since Sep 4.

**Bull thesis:** Hold the position, trail at 42.75, target 45.15 / 46.40. The primary trend is alive, fundamentals are improving, and the company is buying its own stock at these levels.

# Bear Researcher

## Round 1

I hear the 200-day argument, but let's be honest about what's happening on the tape right now. ACT has **failed at 46.40 twice** — Aug 26 and Sep 1–2 — and every rally attempt since has been sold. We're now below the 50-day SMA (44.25), below the 10-day EMA (44.18), and below VWMA (44.31). That's not consolidation; that's distribution.

MACD crossed bearish with the histogram at −0.27 and widening. The signal line is still positive at +0.13, but momentum is clearly rolling over. Sep 15 saw 23,380 shares traded on a drop to 43.00 — that's real selling pressure, not a buyback offset.

And let's talk about those "improving fundamentals." Profit margin is 1.66%. Operating margin is 1.81%. This is a **low-margin distributor** where a 50-basis-point squeeze on gross margin wipes out the entire EPS growth story. Q1 cash from financing was −35.5M PLN — they're funding buybacks with debt or draining reserves. Cash went from 70.5M (Jun 2025) to 21.3M (Dec 2025) before recovering to 57M — that's lumpy, not fortress.

The buyback narrative is a double-edged sword. Yes, they're buying — but at what pace? Two ESPI disclosures don't tell us volume or price. Meanwhile, a shareholder just dropped below 5% — someone with real information is reducing, not adding.

Price is sitting just 1.2 PLN above the Sep 4 low at 42.75. One bad day and you're through invalidation. ATR is 1.23 — that's one session of normal volatility to breach the stop. The risk/reward from 43.95 to 42.75 (−2.7%) vs 45.15 (+2.7%) is symmetric at best, and the momentum is against you.

**Bear thesis:** Trim or exit. The tactical trend has turned, margins are structurally thin, and 42.75 is one ATR away. Don't wait for a break — the first close under 50-day with dead MACD is the warning.
