## Round 1

The bull is cherry-picking a five-day MACD bounce after a brutal 15% drawdown that happened *despite* a +44% earnings beat. That's the tell — when great news produces a selloff, the market is telling you something the headlines won't: this is priced for perfection and the market doesn't believe the beat is repeatable.

Let's talk cash. Cash went from 122.8 mln to 19.4 mln in three quarters. Free cash flow TTM is **negative 411.7 mln PLN**. Operating cash was only 81.7 mln in Q2 against 90 mln in investing outflows. They're burning cash to fund undisclosed projects with no revenue visibility. That's not "investing in the pipeline" — that's a balance sheet squeeze waiting for a miss.

Technically, the "base" at 228–230 was carved on panic volume (891K shares on Sep 4, 655K on Sep 9). Today's pullback from 248 to 245 on light volume isn't "healthy consolidation" — it's failure at resistance. We're still below the 200 SMA. The August gap at 258–265 is overhead supply from trapped longs.

The licensing revenue story is speculative — "undisclosed projects" is CFO code for "trust us." Wishlist sign-ups don't equal revenue; Cyberpunk 2077 had massive pre-launch hype too.

For an existing holder at 249, you're underwater with no clear catalyst until the next earnings print. Risk/reward from here is poor: 7% downside to 228 vs uncertain upside into a known resistance zone. Trim or set a hard stop — don't let a dead-cat bounce become a larger loss.
