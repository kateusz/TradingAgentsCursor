# Bear Researcher

## Round 1

I respect the earnings beat, but the bear case has stronger weight for anyone entering or holding at 240 PLN.

**The selloff wasn't irrational — it was repricing.** CDR fell 12% post-earnings because the market correctly sees that Q2's 80% net margin and 142.9 mln profit are peak-cycle, not sustainable run-rate. Forward P/E of 151x screams "earnings cliff ahead." Q4 2025 revenue was just 74.9 mln PLN — the business is inherently lumpy, and licensing revenue from "unannounced projects" is opaque and unrepeatable by definition.

**Technicals remain bearish.** Price is still below both the 50 SMA (242.5) and 200 SMA (246.7). The "recovery" from 226 to 240 is a dead-cat bounce within a downtrend that started at 297 (52-week high). MACD is still deeply negative (-2.95); the histogram tick to +0.09 is noise, not a trend reversal. Until price reclaims 246.7 on volume, this is a bear market rally.

**Cash is burning.** Cash dropped from 122.8 mln to 19.4 mln in three quarters while investing outflows hit -246 mln in Q1 alone. Management is spending aggressively with no guarantee the secret projects deliver. Operating cash flow of 81.7 mln in Q2 won't cover sustained -90 to -246 mln quarterly investment burns indefinitely.

**Valuation offers no margin of safety.** At 240 PLN with P/E 41x TTM and P/B 6.7x, you're paying a premium for peak earnings in a cyclical business. The 900k wishlist figure is a marketing metric, not revenue. DLC attach rates are uncertain.

**The bull's "accumulation" narrative is wishful thinking.** High volume on down days (891k on Sep 4, 2.1M on Aug 25) shows distribution, not accumulation. The bounce volume is lower than the selloff volume.

**Bottom line:** Risk/reward is poor at 240. Trim or avoid. Support at 226 is fragile; 211 (52-week low) is the real floor. Don't confuse a good company with a good stock at this price.
