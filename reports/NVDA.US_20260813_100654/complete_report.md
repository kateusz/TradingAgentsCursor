# Bear Researcher

## Round 1

I hear the bull enthusiasm, but let's ground this in what the data actually warns about — because the setup at $224 has more cracks than the momentum crowd admits.

**The $500B financing deal is a double-edged sword.** Michael Burry isn't alone — CDS levels peaked last month and remain elevated. When the world's most valuable chip company needs Wall Street to co-fund its customers' data centers, that's not "architecting the stack," that's **capital intensity shifting onto NVDA's balance sheet and reputation**. If any piece of that $500B structure underperforms, NVDA gets hit twice: as equity holder and as ecosystem guarantor.

**Customer vertical integration is happening now, not in 5 years.** TheStreet's reporting on Microsoft building alternatives isn't speculative — MSFT is NVDA's largest customer. When your biggest buyer is paying billions to **not** need you, that's a structural margin threat. This isn't priced into a stock trading 7% below its 52-week high while the entire market knows the story.

**Technicals are extended, not confirmed.** Yes, MACD crossed bullish — but price is hugging the upper Bollinger band at $228 with RSI at 62 approaching resistance. The July low at $190 was a 20% drawdown in weeks. With beta at 2.22 and ATR at $7.51, another $30–$40 flush is one bad headline away. The "recovery" from $190 to $224 already happened — you're buying the rip, not the dip.

**Valuation isn't cheap on any normalized basis.** Forward P/E of 17.5× assumes $12.82 EPS — that's 90% growth from TTM $6.72. Miss one quarter and you're at 25–30× on revised numbers. PEG of 0.60 only works if growth sustains at current pace indefinitely.

**Insider selling continues.** Director Stevens sold $186M+ in June at $210–$222. When insiders sell into your "breakout," pay attention.

The bull case requires everything going right simultaneously. At $224 near resistance with elevated CDS, customer defection risk, and extended technicals, the risk/reward favors caution — not chasing.
