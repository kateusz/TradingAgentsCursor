## Indicator-by-Indicator Analysis

Price at $216.14 sits **$24.44 (10.1%) below** the 50 SMA. The 50 SMA itself has been **rolling over** — it peaked near $241.57 on Aug 31 and is now declining for the first time since the February recovery. This confirms the intermediate trend has flipped bearish.

- **Historical context:** During the February selloff, price traded 15–20% below the 50 SMA before the eventual bounce — suggesting further downside is possible before a durable low forms.

### 2. Long-Term Trend — 200 SMA ($228.08)

The 200 SMA remains **sloping upward** (from $229.60 on Aug 4 to $228.08 now — slight flattening), reflecting the broader recovery from February lows. However, price is now **$11.94 below** this level, having crossed under it during the Aug 31 session.

- **Death cross watch:** The 50 SMA ($240.58) is still **above** the 200 SMA ($228.08), so the golden-cross structure from the spring recovery is intact — but the gap is narrowing rapidly. A confirmed 50/200 death cross would be a significant longer-term bearish signal; monitor whether the 50 SMA crosses below $228 in the coming weeks.
- **Key level:** $228 is the **most important support** in the current setup. A weekly close below $228 would break the long-term uptrend and likely trigger systematic selling.

### 3. Short-Term Momentum — 10 EMA ($227.89)

The 10 EMA has **collapsed from $244.33 (Aug 4) to $227.89 (Sep 2)** — a $16 decline in under a month. Price at $216.14 is **$11.75 below** the 10 EMA, indicating strong short-term bearish momentum.

- **Current signal:** Bearish. The 10 EMA is falling sharply and price shows no sign of catching it.

### 4. MACD Momentum — MACD Line (-5.19)

The MACD turned negative around **Aug 24–25** (MACD: -0.46 → -1.03) and has accelerated to **-5.19** — the most negative reading in the August window. This reflects the speed of the recent decline overwhelming the slower EMA components.

- **Crossover:** MACD crossed below its signal line around Aug 25–26, generating a **bearish sell signal** that remains active.
- **Divergence check:** No bullish divergence is present — price made lower lows (233 → 216) while MACD made lower lows (-0.46 → -5.19), confirming trend integrity to the downside.

### 5. MACD Acceleration — Histogram (-2.75)

The histogram at **-2.75** is the most negative in the dataset period, and the **rate of decline is accelerating** (-0.79 on Aug 28 → -1.16 → -1.94 → -2.51 → -2.75). This indicates bearish momentum is **strengthening**, not exhausting.

- **Capitulation signal to watch:** Histogram extremes often precede short-term bounces. The Feb 2026 low saw histogram readings near -3 to -4 before a multi-week recovery. Current readings are approaching similar capitulation territory.
- **Actionable:** Do not buy into negative-accelerating histogram bars. Wait for the histogram to **contract toward zero** (less negative) for at least 2–3 sessions before considering long entries.

### 6. RSI — Relative Strength Index (31.69)

RSI has fallen from **60.5 (Aug 10)** to **31.69 (Sep 2)**, approaching the traditional **30 oversold threshold**. The decline has been steady rather than cliff-like, suggesting orderly selling rather than panic — which can mean oversold conditions persist longer.

- **Not yet oversold by strict definition** (RSI > 30), but within 1.7 points of it.
- **Trend context:** During the February crash, RSI bottomed near **25–28** before the durable reversal. Current RSI has room to fall further before reaching similar extremes.
- **Caution:** In strong downtrends, RSI can remain below 40 for extended periods. A single oversold reading is insufficient for a buy signal — require RSI divergence (price lower low, RSI higher low) or a reclaim above 40 for confirmation.

### 7. Bollinger Lower Band ($216.30)

Price at $216.14 is **$0.16 below** the lower Bollinger band ($216.30) — effectively **riding the lower band**, a hallmark of strong downtrends. The band itself has dropped sharply from $229.84 (Aug 4) as volatility expanded.

- **Band walk:** In sustained declines, price can "walk" the lower band for many sessions. The Feb 2026 selloff saw 5+ consecutive closes at or below the lower band before a bounce.
- **Bollinger squeeze potential:** The upper band has collapsed from $252.75 to $257.35 (widening spread due to the drop, not a squeeze). The bands are **expanding downward**, confirming a volatility breakout to the downside rather than a consolidation setup.
- **Support:** The lower band at $216.30 is immediate support. A close **below $216** on volume would signal continuation toward the February lows near $190–$195.

### 8. ATR — Average True Range ($8.69)

ATR has risen from **$7.11 (Aug 4)** to **$8.69 (Sep 2)** — a **22% increase** in measured volatility. This is significant for risk management:

- **Stop-loss sizing:** A 1.5× ATR stop below entry would be ~$13 below price (~$203), while a 2× ATR stop would be ~$199. These are wide — reflecting the current environment's unpredictability.
- **Position sizing:** With ATR at 4.0% of price ($8.69 / $216.14), position sizes should be reduced proportionally vs. the low-volatility July period (ATR was ~$7, or 2.7% of price at $258).
- **Volatility regime:** ATR peaked near $9.17 on Aug 31 (the crash day) and remains elevated, indicating the market has not yet returned to a calm state.
