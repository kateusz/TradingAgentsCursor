## Closing Argument

The bull will say I am fighting the tape — SpaceX, AWS through 2028, Volta, SMCI, Huang's energy TAM. They will say Burry is losing on his short and AMD is merely adequate. They will say forward P/E of 16.4x is cheap for the best compounder in the market.

My response: **every late-cycle semiconductor winner looks like the best compounder in the market at the peak.** NVIDIA's operational excellence is real. The question is not whether NVIDIA is a great company — it is whether a **$5.13 trillion** market cap at **31.6x trailing earnings**, with **inventory doubling**, **insiders selling near highs**, **customers under capex pressure**, and **beta 2.22** offers acceptable risk-adjusted returns for *new capital*.

I do not need AI demand to collapse to make the bear case. I need it to **normalize** — growth decelerating from 85% to 40%, margins slipping from 75% to 65%, one hyperscaler guiding capex down, deployment bottlenecks converting orders into inventory. In that scenario, forward EPS of $12.89 becomes a fantasy, the multiple compresses toward 20x on real earnings, and a **$130–$150** stock is not absurd — it is a garden-variety semiconductor derating on a beta-2 mega-cap.

The market is pricing perfection. Perfection is the most crowded trade in the history of equity markets. Crowded trades do not end gently.

**Bear recommendation:** Avoid new long exposure at current levels. Existing holders should tighten risk management (stops below **$203** per technical analysis), hedge tail risk given index concentration, and watch inventory days and hyperscaler capex guidance as the two triggers that convert this from a "debate" into a "drawdown."
