# Bear Researcher

# Bear Research: ACT.WA

## Round 1

I respect the uptrend, but the risk-reward from current levels skews unfavorably for new money and demands caution for existing holders.

**The easy money has been made.** A 70% rally in twelve months from 27 to 46 PLN means late entrants are buying at the top of a parabolic move. The stock is only 4.4% below its all-time high — this is not a dip, it's a rounding error from the peak.

**Momentum has turned.** MACD is firmly below its signal line with a negative histogram for three consecutive weeks. Price is below the 10 EMA (45.20) and VWMA (45.54). These are distribution signals, not consolidation markers. Volume on down days (12,995 on Aug 28) exceeded recovery volume — sellers are more aggressive than buyers.

**Valuation is no longer a bargain.** P/B of 1.48 means you're paying nearly 1.5x book for a low-margin distributor (1.64% net margin). The PEG of 0.57 looks attractive but is based on trailing growth that may not repeat — H1 revenue annualizes to 2.9B vs TTM 3.06B, suggesting deceleration.

**Structural risks are underappreciated.** Debt/equity of 1.46 is elevated for a thin-margin business. Cash swung from 70M to 21M to 57M in three quarters — volatile working capital management. A single bad quarter in electronics distribution (inventory write-downs, FX, supplier issues) could compress the already-thin margin to zero.

**Buybacks are not a silver bullet.** Treasury purchases at 44–46 PLN support price mechanically but don't change the underlying business economics. If growth stalls, buybacks just delay the inevitable re-rating.

**Technical invalidation is close.** A break below 43.64 (50 SMA) opens a path to 42.30 and potentially 40 PLN — that's a 10% drawdown from here. With ATR at 1.08, that move could happen in 5–7 sessions.

The bull cites improving ROE and EPS growth. But ROE improvement from buyback-driven EPS inflation is less impressive than organic margin expansion — and margins are barely moving (1.41% to 1.64% over four quarters).

**Bottom line:** Tighten stops, don't add. The risk of a deeper correction outweighs the marginal upside to 48–50 PLN from here. Protect capital at 44.00 or below.
