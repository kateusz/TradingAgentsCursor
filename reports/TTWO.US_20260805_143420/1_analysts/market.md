## Executive Summary

TTWO has spent the past year in a wide, event-driven trading range after a catastrophic earnings-driven gap in late January 2026. The stock recovered from a low near **$188** in February to a July peak of **$265.94**, but has since pulled back roughly **6.3%** from that high. The key battleground is the **$234–$242** zone where the 50 SMA, VWMA, and Bollinger middle converge.

## Indicator-by-Indicator Analysis

### 1. 50 SMA (close_50_sma): **$234.34** — Bullish, Rising

The 50 SMA has climbed steadily from **$227.09** (Jul 6) to **$234.34** (Aug 4), gaining ~$7.25 in one month. Price at $240.22 trades **~2.5% above** the 50 SMA, confirming the medium-term uptrend that began with the June rally.

- **Support test ahead:** The pullback from $258 has not yet tagged the 50 SMA. A dip to $234–$235 would be a textbook retest of rising support.
- **Invalidation:** A daily close below $230 (50 SMA minus ~1.5 ATR) would signal medium-term trend damage.
- **Historical context:** The 50 SMA acted as resistance throughout March–May 2026 ($230–$240 zone) before the June breakout converted it to support.

### 2. 200 SMA (close_200_sma): **$229.60** — Long-Term Uptrend Intact

Despite the January crash, TTWO never closed below the 200 SMA during the lookback period. The 200 SMA has been slowly rising ($230.92 → $229.60 is actually a slight flattening, reflecting the Feb drawdown still being digested).

- Price trades **~4.6% above** the 200 SMA—comfortable margin.
- The **50/200 SMA spread** (~$4.74) remains positive, confirming no death cross.
- Strategic investors can treat $229–$230 as the "line in the sand" for the year-long bullish structure.

### 3. 10 EMA (close_10_ema): **$242.33** — Short-Term Bearish Bias

The 10 EMA has rolled over from **$248.66** (Jul 8) to **$242.33** (Aug 4). Price at $240.22 is **below** the 10 EMA for the first time since the late-June rally, signaling short-term momentum loss.

- The EMA slope turned negative around Jul 24–27 when price failed to hold $244.
- **Reclaim trigger:** A close above $243–$244 (10 EMA + Bollinger middle) would neutralize the short-term bearish tilt.
- **Caution:** In choppy post-earnings environments, the 10 EMA produces whipsaws; pair with the 50 SMA for confirmation.

### 4. MACD Histogram (macdh): **-0.15** — Momentum Fading

The histogram tells the full MACD story without needing separate macd/macds lines:

- **Jul 6–10:** Histogram positive and expanding (peak +2.79 on Jul 6) — strong bullish momentum during the rally.
- **Jul 13–24:** Histogram compressed toward zero, then crossed negative on Jul 24 (**-2.55**) — bearish crossover confirmed.
- **Jul 27–Aug 3:** Brief positive spike (+0.14 on Aug 3) on the Jul 27 bounce to $243.95 — failed recovery attempt.
- **Aug 4:** Back to **-0.15** — momentum resuming downward.

**Actionable read:** The MACD histogram bearish crossover on Jul 24 preceded the slide from $243 to $240. Traders should watch for histogram re-expansion above zero as a buy signal, or deepening negative readings as confirmation of a move toward the 50 SMA.

### 5. RSI: **49.92** — Neutral, Momentum Normalized

RSI has completed a full cycle:

- **Overbought:** RSI hit **74.3** on Jul 6–7 at the price peak — a reliable leading indicator of the subsequent pullback.
- **Oversold probe:** RSI dipped to **42.0** on Jul 23 during the $230 low — not extreme, suggesting the pullback was orderly, not panicked.
- **Current:** **49.92** — dead center; neither bulls nor bears have control.

**Divergence watch:** Price made a lower high on Aug 4 ($246.25) vs. Jul 28 ($248.70), while RSI also made a lower high (49.9 vs. 58.5). This mild bearish divergence supports the consolidation/pullback thesis. A bullish divergence would require price to make a lower low with RSI holding above 42.

- Price is hugging the middle band — classic consolidation. A close below $241 targets the lower band near $230; a close above $243 targets $252–$255.
- The lower band at $229.84 aligns almost perfectly with the 200 SMA ($229.60) — a high-confluence support zone.

### 7. ATR: **$7.11** — Moderate, Contracting Volatility

ATR has declined from **$8.63** (Jul 6, during the rally) to **$7.11** (Aug 4) — a **~18% contraction**. This is consistent with the Bollinger squeeze.

**Risk management applications:**

- **Stop-loss (long):** 1.5× ATR below entry = ~$10.67 buffer. For a long at $240, stop at ~$229 (aligns with 200 SMA/lower Bollinger).
- **Stop-loss (short):** 1.5× ATR above entry. For a short at $240, stop at ~$251.
- **Position sizing:** With ATR at $7.11 on a $240 stock, daily expected range is ~3.0% — moderate for a large-cap gaming name. Size accordingly.
- **Note:** ATR spiked above $8 during every major event (Jan 30 gap, Feb selloff, May 22 drop, Jun 18 rally). Watch for ATR expansion as a breakout/breakdown warning.

### 8. VWMA: **$240.79** — Volume-Weighted Fair Value

Price ($240.22) sits **$0.57 below** VWMA ($240.79) — essentially at fair value on a volume-weighted basis.

- VWMA peaked at **$247.66** (Jul 17) and has declined to $240.79, tracking the distribution from July highs.
- The Jun 18 rally day (7.2M volume, +$16) and Jun 29–30 sessions pulled VWMA sharply higher, confirming institutional accumulation during the rally.
- **Aug 4 volume** of 2.1M is above the ~1.5M average but not climactic — selling is orderly, not capitulative.
- A reclaim of VWMA with expanding volume would signal buyers returning; sustained trade below VWMA with rising volume would confirm further distribution.
