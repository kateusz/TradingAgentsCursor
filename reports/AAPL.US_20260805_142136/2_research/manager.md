**Recommendation**: Hold

**Rationale**: Apple (AAPL.US) presents a genuinely contested setup where neither the bull nor the bear thesis dominates decisively enough to warrant aggressive directional action. The bull case rests on real, accelerating fundamentals: fiscal Q3 2026 revenue grew 16.4% YoY to $109.4B, EPS rose 28.7% to $2.02, and free cash flow hit a record $31.9B (+30.7% YoY). Margins remain elite (27.6% net, 32.6% operating), TTM FCF of ~$108B funds $25.1B in quarterly buybacks, and structural growth vectors (India crossing $10B in annual sales, Foxconn record July revenue +54% YoY) confirm the hardware pipeline is healthy. The 10% post-earnings drawdown to $309.38 — sitting on the 50-day SMA ($309.61) and above the long-term 200 SMA by 11.2% — looks like a sentiment dislocation after the best June quarter in company history, with the September iPhone launch as a near-term catalyst.

The bear case, however, is validated by market behavior and forward-looking risk. The selloff was not a misunderstanding of backward results; it was a guidance-driven repricing at 34.8x trailing earnings and a PEG of 2.46, where the market explicitly looked past the beat. Technical evidence confirms institutional distribution: July 31's -9.3% crash on 132.5M shares (nearly double average volume), price $11 below the 10 EMA with a failed reclaim, MACD histogram deepening to -3.52 over four sessions, and a weak recovery on declining volume. The market analyst assigns moderate-to-high probability to bearish continuation if $302 (Bollinger lower band) breaks. Relative underperformance versus the AI-led Nasdaq rally, CEO transition uncertainty, the "renting not building" AI strategy that caps multiple expansion, OpenAI litigation headline risk, and balance-sheet watch items (inventory +88% YoY, receivables elevated, current ratio ~1.0) add medium-term overhang. India at ~2% of TTM revenue cannot offset a guidance miss near-term, and the $2.2B tariff refund is a one-time tailwind, not a recurring growth driver.

After weighing both sides against the full analyst suite: fundamentals rate Apple as a positive-quality franchise at a fair-to-rich valuation; sentiment is bifurcated and guidance-sensitive but not capitulated (RSI 44.7, not yet reclaimed 50); news confirms a "beat and guide down" dynamic with demand intact but forward expectations deteriorating; and the market technician explicitly recommends waiting for confirmation (hold $302 + RSI > 50 + volume expansion) before initiating new long exposure. The conservative long entry conditions outlined in the market report are not yet met. Existing holders are not facing a fundamental breakdown warranting exit, but new capital lacks a favorable risk/reward at current levels without technical or guidance confirmation. This is the balanced case where maintaining the current position with defined risk parameters is the appropriate stance — not adding into unresolved distribution, and not selling a record-earnings compounder into support.
