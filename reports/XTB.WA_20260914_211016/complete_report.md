## Indicator selection (8)

XTB.WA is in a **violent post-parabolic correction** after a year-long advance that peaked near 187 in late August. The useful question is not “is the long-term trend still up?” (it is) but “has the medium-term trend broken, and is this a washout or the start of a deeper unwind?” The eight indicators below answer that without stacking redundant oscillators.

1. **close_200_sma** — Long-term trend anchor. Price remains far above the 200-day; this is the only structural bull filter still intact and the map for how much room a true trend failure would still have.
2. **close_50_sma** — Medium-term trend and first dynamic resistance. Price lost this average on 10 September; whether it recaptures or rejects here decides if the correction is a pause or a regime change.
3. **close_10_ema** — Short-term trend and bounce filter. Price sliced through it on 2 September and has not reclaimed it; any tradable bounce should be judged against this line, not against the August highs.
4. **macd** — Momentum regime. The line has flipped from strongly positive (~10–12 in mid/late August) through a zero-line cross after 10 September. That is a genuine trend-change signal, not just a dip in a still-rising MACD.
5. **macdh** — Timing and exhaustion inside that MACD regime. Histogram turned negative in mid-August (momentum peaked before price) and is deeply negative now, with a hint of deceleration off the 10 September trough. Complements MACD without duplicating the signal line.
6. **rsi** — Overbought/oversold and divergence. RSI spent late August above 70, then collapsed to the mid-30s. It is **not yet oversold**; that gap matters for anyone hunting a V-bottom.
7. **boll_lb** — Location inside a volatility expansion. Price tagged and briefly pierced the lower band on 10 September. With bands this wide, riding the lower band is as plausible as an immediate mean-reversion bounce.
8. **atr** — Risk sizing. Daily range has expanded to ~6.9 PLN (~4.7% of spot). Stops and position size must be rebuilt off this number, not off the quieter July–early-August ATR.
