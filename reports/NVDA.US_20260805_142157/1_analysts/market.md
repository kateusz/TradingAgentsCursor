## Executive Summary

NVDA.US remains in a **structurally bullish long-term uptrend**, trading at **$211.94** (August 4 close)—approximately **10% below** the cycle high of **$235.47** (May 14, 2026) but **~30% above** the March 2026 trough near **$164.98**. Over the past year, the stock advanced from ~$178 to current levels, with the dominant narrative being a powerful AI-driven rally through May 2026 followed by a multi-month consolidation and a sharp late-July shakeout.

The current setup is a **short-term recovery bounce within a medium-term range** ($190–$236). Price has reclaimed the 50-day SMA and 10-day EMA after dipping to **$190.01** (July 29), and the MACD histogram has turned positive—signaling early momentum recovery. RSI has rebounded from oversold territory (37.97 on July 29) to neutral-bullish (56.94). However, price remains below the upper Bollinger Band ($217.23) and well below the May peak, suggesting the rally is corrective rather than a confirmed breakout to new highs.

Traders should watch the **$215–$218 zone** (upper Bollinger band and prior resistance) as the immediate ceiling, and **$205–$203** (50 SMA / 10 EMA cluster) as the first support band on pullbacks. A sustained break above $218 with volume would reopen the path toward $225–$235; failure to hold $203 would signal a retest of the $190–$195 July lows.
