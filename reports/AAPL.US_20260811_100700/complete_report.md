# Bull Researcher

Jefferies' $263.66 target implies roughly 25x forward earnings for the best-brand, best-margin hardware ecosystem on earth — that's not analysis, that's capitulation math.

**The margin scare is already being managed.** The memory-cost story is real, but Apple has three levers the market is ignoring: (1) it is reportedly **testing CXMT memory chips** to diversify away from DRAM price spikes — a textbook margin-defense move; (2) Apple's pricing power is the strongest in the industry — Cook's "expect higher prices" comment is not a threat to demand, it's a statement that Apple can pass cost through where others cannot; (3) services (~25% of revenue, ~75% gross margin) is growing steadily and shields the P&L from hardware component swings.

The failed break below $300 with a quick reclaim is classic stop-hunt behavior.

**The upgrade-cycle argument is backwards.** Every iPhone transition brings a period of "product cycle doom" headlines followed by record quarters. The stock dropped ~7.5% into the launch of iPhone 16 in Sept 2025 ($255 → $237) and then rallied ~35% to $344 within 10 months. The **iPhone 18 launch is the setup, not the problem** — this is precisely the window where long-term investors and swing traders should be positioning before the December holiday quarter ($143.8B revenue quarter, EPS $2.84) prints.

**Where does the bull land on levels?** We are in a swing correction within a primary uptrend. Entry zone $303–310 is the low-risk buy window; if the 50-day SMA is reclaimed above $313, the path to $323 (pre-crash base) and $340+ reopens. The all-glass iPhone may be dead — but the iPhone itself, the services engine, the buyback ($25.1B in Q3 alone) and the AI cycle are very much alive.

**Summary:** The bear is trading headlines; the bull trades the balance sheet and the trend. FCF of $107B, gross margin of 50%, 27% net income growth, and a rising 200-day MA do not support a sell. Support at $300 held once; it will be bought again. **AAPL at $308 is a buy, not a bail.**
