# Research Manager Decision: Genpact Limited (G.US)

**Report date:** 2026-08-05  
**Ticker:** G.US (Genpact Limited)  
**Role:** Research Manager — Debate Synthesis & Investment Plan

---

**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: The bull and bear cases both land on verifiable facts, but the weight of evidence favors a constructive — not aggressive — stance. Genpact trades at a trailing P/E of 10.8x and forward P/E of ~8x on $5.16B revenue, 23.1% ROE, 15.4% operating margins, and ~$712M TTM free cash flow. Q1 2026 EPS grew 18% YoY ($0.73 → $0.86) while revenue grew 6.7%, confirming operating leverage and buyback accretion (~3% share count reduction). At ~$35.79, the stock sits in the lower half of its 52-week range ($26.85–$48.64) and roughly 6% below the declining 200-day SMA ($37.84), meaning much of the growth-skepticism that drove the 36% drawdown from December 2025 highs is already embedded in price.

The bear case correctly identifies unresolved risks that prevent a full **Buy** rating. Q1 2026 free cash flow turned negative (-$55M) on a -$225M working-capital swing while the company returned $102M to shareholders via buybacks and dividends — a pattern the fundamentals analyst flagged as requiring Q2 normalization. Net debt rose 46% YoY to $958M, partly funding a $350M Q4 2025 debt issuance used for repurchases at prices above today's quote. Forward consensus EPS of $4.48 implies ~35% growth from TTM $3.32 against 7% revenue growth; the PEG of 1.16 confirms the stock is fairly valued on growth-adjusted metrics, not a deep mispricing. Technically, the market analyst frames the setup as a "high-momentum recovery rally inside a broader downtrend — not yet a confirmed trend reversal": price is above the 50 SMA ($31.11) and 10 EMA ($34.04) but below the 200 SMA, with RSI at 68.5 (cooled from 76.1 overbought), MACD histogram contracting from its peak, and volume fading during consolidation at $35–36.

Sentiment and news offer modest support but not conviction. Composite sentiment scores 2.8/5 — mildly positive, low visibility — anchored by a single Vulcan Value Partners Q2 2026 letter asking whether Genpact is an "underestimated force in AI disruption." The news analyst correctly notes this is a sentiment signal, not an operational catalyst: no contract wins, AI revenue disclosures, or earnings commentary appeared in the seven-day window. The AI thesis remains narrative-driven; the bear's point that Genpact competes in commoditized IT services against Accenture, Cognizant, and Wipro at mid-tier scale is fair, though the bull's counter — that enterprise AI implementation demand flows through services partners regardless of hyperscaler capex friction — has macro plausibility without current proof in Genpact's reported numbers.

The decisive factor is asymmetry at current valuation. Downside is cushioned by a 10.8x trailing P/E, 2.13% dividend yield, aggressive buybacks (~6% of market cap TTM), 0.58 beta, and cash of $578M with net debt/EBITDA of ~1.1x — the company is not distressed. Upside requires Q2 2026 earnings (~August 2026) to confirm FCF normalization and progress toward forward EPS, plus a sustained reclaim of the 200 SMA at $37.84. The bull's fair-value range of $48–54 (12–15x forward EPS) is achievable only if both earnings delivery and multiple expansion occur; the bear's $28–34 structural re-rating scenario activates if Q2 FCF disappoints and forward estimates are cut. Given imminent Q2 as a binary proof point, **Overweight** — gradually increasing exposure on confirmed evidence — is more appropriate than **Buy** (full conviction before Q2 validation) or **Hold** (which understates the valuation discount and improving EPS trajectory). **Underweight** or **Sell** would ignore that the market has already repriced the stock down 36% and that TTM fundamentals remain solid despite one weak cash-flow quarter.

**Strategic Actions**:

1. **Build exposure gradually, not at market.** Do not chase at $35.79 near resistance ($36.93 upper Bollinger / $37.84 200 SMA). Preferred entry zones: (a) pullback to $34.00–$34.50 (10 EMA / 1× ATR support band), or (b) breakout entry on a daily close above $36.93 with volume ≥ 2.5M shares, targeting $37.84–$40.00. Size positions for elevated volatility (ATR ~$1.35, ~3.8% daily range).

2. **Set a hard stop at $31.11 (50 SMA).** A daily close below this level invalidates the medium-term recovery structure identified by the market analyst and reopens the June low zone ($26.85–$28.25). This is ~13% below current price and defines maximum acceptable loss on new positions.

3. **Treat Q2 2026 earnings as the thesis gate (~August 2026).** Before adding beyond a starter position, verify: (a) FCF recovery from Q1's -$55M (operating cash flow and receivables normalization), (b) net debt stable or declining from $958M, (c) forward guidance consistent with $4.48 consensus EPS. If FCF remains negative for a second consecutive quarter and debt-funded buybacks continue, downgrade to **Hold** and trim to benchmark weight.

4. **Use the $36.93–$37.84 resistance band for tactical decisions.** Existing holders with overweight positions can trim 25–33% of incremental shares into this zone if price approaches resistance without volume confirmation, preserving core exposure for a potential 200 SMA reclaim. Do not add new shares above $37.84 without a confirmed daily close above the 200 SMA.

5. **Monitor sentiment and peer catalysts.** Watch for follow-on 13F filings echoing Vulcan's AI positioning, Accenture/Cognizant earnings as sector read-throughs, and clarification of StockStory's watchlist classification (constructive vs. "risky" pick). Absence of additional institutional validation after Q2 would weaken the AI narrative premium.

6. **Risk-adjusted target and horizon.** Base-case 6–12 month target: $42–48 (assuming partial forward EPS delivery and modest multiple expansion to 10–12x). Bear-case floor if thesis breaks: $28–31 (50 SMA failure → June low retest). Reward-to-risk from a $34 entry with $31 stop and $45 target is approximately 3.7:1 — adequate for an **Overweight** stance with staged entry.
