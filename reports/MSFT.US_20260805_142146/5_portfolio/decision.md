**Executive Summary**: Maintain current MSFT exposure; do not initiate or add at $492–493. The earnings-validated AI platform thesis and institutional breakout justify continued ownership for existing longs, but RSI at 79, price 3% above the upper Bollinger Band, and asymmetric near-term risk/reward (+3.5% to $510 vs. −5.8% to $464 / −8.5% gap fill to $451) make chasing the four-session +26.2% rally imprudent. **Investment Thesis**: Microsoft remains a fortress compounder in an AI infrastructure investment phase, not a broken narrative. Q2 FY2026 revenue reached $90.0B (+17.8% YoY), Microsoft Cloud $59.3B (+27% YoY), operating margins 45.1%, and operating cash flow accelerated 30% YoY to $55.4B even as quarterly capex doubled to $35.8B — confirming cash generation is strengthening while capital is redeployed into demand AWS describes as "spoken for through 2028." The July 30 gap on 110.2M shares (~3.7× average volume) was institutional re-rating; sentiment has flipped from "AI victim" to "AI platform winner." Balance sheet quality is elite ($442B equity, $76.7B cash, $73.0B current deferred revenue). The bear case is timing and valuation, not business deterioration: 27.2× trailing P/E, PEG 1.59, ~1.8% TTM FCF yield, quarterly FCF compressed 24% to $19.6B, Michael Burry's exit, and no insider buying cluster at $493 signal limited near-term upside. Aggressive risk argues for tactical adds now to avoid missing $500–530; conservative risk urges 15–20% trim into extension. Both extremes are partially right — the business warrants overweight strategic conviction, but the price at $493 does not warrant immediate action. Hold is the disciplined synthesis: conviction in the thesis, discipline on entry.

**Price Target**: $510 near-term resistance (first profit-taking zone); $520–530 medium-term if $500 breaks on volume
