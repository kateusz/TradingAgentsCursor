## Executive Summary

Apple (AAPL.US) is in a **short-term corrective phase within a structurally bullish long-term uptrend**. After rallying from ~$275 in early June to an all-time high of $340.08 on July 28, the stock suffered a sharp -9.3% single-session decline on July 31 ($333.43 → $308.91) on record volume of 132.5M shares—nearly double the 20-day average. Price has since stabilized near the 50-day SMA (~$309.61) but remains below all short-term moving averages and volume-weighted benchmarks.

The current setup is a **high-stakes support test**: bulls need to hold the 50 SMA and Bollinger lower band (~$302) to preserve the medium-term uptrend; failure opens a path toward the 200 SMA (~$278) while still keeping the primary bull market intact.

## Trend Analysis

### Long-Term (200 SMA): Bullish Structure Intact

- **200 SMA:** $278.25 (as of 2026-08-04)
- **Price premium:** +11.2% above the 200 SMA
- Over the past year, AAPL advanced from $202 to $340—a +68% move. The 200 SMA has risen steadily from ~$271 (July 6) to $278, confirming a sustained primary uptrend.
- The July 31 crash did **not** threaten the long-term trend framework. Even a retest of the 200 SMA would represent a ~10% further decline—painful but historically normal within bull markets.

### Medium-Term (50 SMA): Critical Inflection Point

- **50 SMA:** $309.61
- **Price vs. 50 SMA:** -0.07% (essentially flat—within $0.23)
- The 50 SMA has been rising aggressively ($294 on July 6 → $309.61 now), reflecting the strong May–July rally. Price closed at $309.38 on August 4, making this the **most important near-term level**.
- **Historical context:** During the January 2026 correction (low $246.24), price never breached the 50 SMA from above on a sustained basis before recovering. The current test is the first meaningful 50 SMA challenge since the June breakout.
- **Actionable:** A daily close below $305 (giving ~1.5% buffer below the 50 SMA) would signal medium-term trend damage. Holds above $309 with rising volume would suggest absorption of the July 31 supply.

### Short-Term (10 EMA): Bearish Momentum

- **10 EMA:** $320.45
- **Price vs. 10 EMA:** -3.5% below
- The 10 EMA peaked near $331 on July 30 and has collapsed to $320.45 as price fell. The gap between price and the 10 EMA widened from near-zero (July 28–29) to -$11, indicating **accelerating short-term weakness**.
- The August 4 bounce ($303.42 → $309.38, +2.0%) failed to reclaim the 10 EMA—classic "dead cat bounce" behavior until proven otherwise.
- **Actionable:** A close above $320 (10 EMA) on expanding volume would be the first constructive short-term signal. Until then, rallies are likely to be sold into.
