## Executive Summary

Genpact Limited is a global IT services and business process management company trading at a notable discount to its own recent history and to typical technology-services multiples. At a trailing P/E of **10.8x** and forward P/E of **8.0x**, the stock screens as inexpensive relative to its earnings power, especially given **23.1% ROE**, **15.4% operating margins**, and **~$712M TTM free cash flow**. The share price sits well below its 52-week high ($48.64) and 200-day moving average ($38.15), suggesting the market is pricing in skepticism about growth or execution risk.

Revenue is growing at a mid-single-digit pace (~7% YoY in the latest quarter), with EPS accelerating ($0.86 diluted in Q1 2026 vs. $0.73 a year earlier). The primary near-term watchpoint is **Q1 FY2026 free cash flow**, which turned negative (-$55M) due to a large working-capital outflow (-$225M), a reversal from consistently strong quarterly FCF in prior periods. Balance-sheet leverage has risen (net debt ~$958M vs. ~$463M a year ago), partly reflecting a $350M long-term debt issuance in Q4 2025. Management continues to return capital via buybacks (~$70M/quarter) and dividends (~$32M/quarter).

**Bottom line for traders:** Genpact offers a value-oriented setup with solid profitability, shareholder returns, and a defensive beta (0.58), but requires monitoring of cash conversion and debt trends. Insider activity is dominated by routine compensation grants and planned sales — no strong insider-buying signal.

## Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Value entry with catalyst watch:** At ~$36 (implied from P/E × EPS), Genpact trades at a significant discount to its 52-week high and 200-day MA. Consider entry on confirmation of Q2 FCF normalization and debt stabilization.

2. **Monitor Q2 2026 earnings (expected ~Aug 2026):** Key metrics: (a) free cash flow recovery, (b) receivables days outstanding, (c) net debt trajectory, (d) forward guidance vs. $4.48 consensus EPS.

3. **Forward P/E of 8x is the bull case anchor:** If Genpact delivers on forward EPS, fair value at a 12x P/E would be ~$54 — ~50% upside. Failure to deliver compresses the thesis.

4. **Defensive positioning:** Low beta (0.58) and 2.1% dividend yield support holding through volatility, but limited upside without growth re-acceleration.

5. **Insider activity is not a buy signal:** Routine selling dominates; do not interpret officer sales as incremental bearishness, but absence of insider buying means no conviction signal from management.

6. **Debt-funded buybacks:** The $350M debt issuance to fund repurchases increases financial risk. If FCF remains weak for 2+ quarters, consider reducing position size.

7. **Compare to peers:** Cross-reference P/E, revenue growth, and FCF yield against peers (e.g., Accenture, Cognizant, Wipro, EXL) before sizing position — Genpact's discount may reflect company-specific or size-related factors.
