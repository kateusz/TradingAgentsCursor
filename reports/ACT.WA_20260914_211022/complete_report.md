### 3. Holder falls below 5% of votes

One komunikat is a **notification that a shareholder’s stake fell below 5% of total votes**:

- https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/ACTION-S-A-Zawiadomienie-o-zmniejszeniu-udzialu-ponizej-progu-5-ogolnej-liczby-glosow-w-Spolce-9195155.html

Under Polish public-company rules this is a **mandatory threshold crossing** (Art. 69-type disclosure). The bundle does not name the holder, the new percentage, or whether the move was a sale, a donation, or a corporate reorganisation.

**Why this is almost certainly selling, not a mechanical capital-reduction effect:** Cancelling treasury shares **reduces** the denominator and **raises** remaining holders’ voting percentages. A crossing **below** 5% therefore implies the holder’s **absolute** (or at least relative-to-others) position was cut — typically by **disposal**.

**Trading implication:**

- **Supply risk:** A former 5%+ holder reducing can print as block or drip selling. That can **offset the buyback bid** and produce a choppy, two-sided tape.
- **Ownership signal:** Loss of a disclosed 5%+ name reduces a visible “anchor” holder. Depending on who it was (financial vs. strategic), this can mean lower governance overlay or simply portfolio rebalancing.
- **Information gap:** Without the holder identity and residual stake, do not treat this as a full exit. It is a **threshold event**, not a complete story.

**Action:** Read the ESPI for **who**, **from/to %**, and **date of the transaction**. If the residual is just under 5%, further sales may continue without another immediate 5% notice until 10%/other thresholds. If it was a clean cut from well above 5% to a small residual, the bulk of the overhang may already have traded.
