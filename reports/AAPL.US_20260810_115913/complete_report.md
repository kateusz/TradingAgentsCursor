# Bear Researcher

## Round 1

The bull case sounds comforting until you look at what actually happened: Apple dropped 10% in a single session on the highest volume in a year, and the "recovery" is a limp 1.4% bounce on declining volume. That's not accumulation — that's a dead-cat bounce.

**The earnings guidance cut is the story, not the headline EPS.** Management explicitly guided weaker, and the market repriced instantly. You don't gap down 10% on 132M shares and then just "bounce back" because buybacks exist. The Jul 31 candle broke the uptrend channel that carried AAPL from $226 to $344 — a 52% rally undone in sentiment overnight.

**Memory costs aren't "priced in" — they're accelerating.** The CXMT testing story confirms Apple is scrambling for supply. When the world's most powerful buyer starts sourcing from Chinese chipmakers amid a "100-year flood," that's not agility — that's margin compression incoming. Gross margin at 48.6% has nowhere to go but down if component costs spike 20-30%. At a 36x P/E, even a 200bps margin miss triggers multiple compression.

**The technical picture is deteriorating, not recovering.** Price is below the 10 EMA ($316.81), below VWMA ($320.14), below Bollinger middle ($323.43). MACD bearish crossover is confirmed with a deeply negative histogram. The only thing holding is the 50 SMA — and that's a single line of defense against a retest of $300 (Bollinger lower band at $300.84). One more bad headline and $308 support crumbles.

**Insider selling was aggressive, not routine.** Levinson dumped 250,000 shares at $284-285 in May. Cook sold 65,000 shares at $251-256 in April. These aren't 10b5-1 dribbles — they're meaningful exits by people who know the margin outlook. The bull cites insider sales as evidence $313 is cheap; I cite them as evidence insiders don't see $340 returning soon.

**CEO succession adds uncertainty premium.** Ternus inherits a margin squeeze, a China market share battle, and an AI strategy that amounts to "take 30% of ChatGPT subscriptions." That's not a growth platform — that's a toll booth with a weakening monopoly.

**For swing traders holding from $331:** you're underwater 5.4% with a stock that can't reclaim its 10 EMA after a week of "recovery." The risk/reward isn't 2:1 to $318 — it's 1:1 to $300 if $308 fails. Cut the position or tighten stops; don't average into a falling knife hoping buybacks save you.
