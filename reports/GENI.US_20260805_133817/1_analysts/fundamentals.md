## Executive Summary

Genius Sports is a sports data, technology, and betting-integrity platform trading at roughly **$2.16B market cap** with a **high-beta (1.88)** profile and a wide 52-week range ($3.83–$13.73). The company is in a **transition phase**: TTM revenue of **~$713M** is growing, but GAAP profitability remains negative (TTM net loss **~$159M**, EPS **-$0.71**). The market is pricing in a turnaround — **forward P/E ~8.3** on forward EPS of **$0.97** — implying expected earnings inflection within the next 12 months.

The balance sheet is **adequately capitalized** (equity **$695M**, cash **$197M**, current ratio **1.60**), with **low traditional debt** (~$31M, largely capital leases). However, **intangibles and goodwill represent ~47% of total assets**, limiting tangible book value to **$214M** (P/B ~3.0 on headline book value of **$2.70/share**).

Cash generation is **volatile but positive on a TTM basis** (free cash flow **~$101M** per fundamentals snapshot), driven by strong Q4 2025 operating cash flow offset by a weak Q1 2026. Traders should weigh the **forward-earnings narrative** against **persistent operating losses**, **seasonal revenue swings**, and **heavy non-cash charges** (stock-based compensation ~$21M/quarter).

No insider transaction data was available for GENI.US in the lookback period, removing a near-term sentiment signal from insider buying or selling.

## Valuation Context & Trading Implications

**Bull case (supports long positioning):**
- Forward P/E of **8.3x** is cheap for a data-rights platform with ~$713M revenue if $0.97 EPS materializes.
- TTM FCF of **~$101M** implies ~**2.1% FCF yield** on $2.16B cap — reasonable for a high-growth infrastructure play transitioning to profit.
- Q4 seasonal strength and deferred revenue backlog support revenue visibility.
- Low debt reduces downside in a risk-off environment.

**Bear case (supports caution or short hedging):**
- Trailing losses are deep; **operating margin still negative** every quarter.
- Q1 2026 showed **margin compression** and **cash burn** — early warning if seasonality disappoints.
- **47% of assets are intangible** — limited liquidation value; P/B of 3x is expensive on tangible book ($214M → implied ~$0.79/share tangible).
- **Beta 1.88** amplifies drawdowns; stock already below 50/200-day averages.
- Dilution and SBC (~$80M+/year) erode per-share economics.
- No insider buying to validate management confidence.

**Key levels to monitor:**
- **Cash trajectory:** sustained quarterly burn below $50M without Q4 offset would stress the $197M cash pile within 2–3 years.
- **Gross margin:** must hold above 25% consistently; Q2 2025's 7.5% shows downside risk.
- **Q4 2026 revenue:** needs to approach $240M+ again to validate the forward earnings bridge.
- **Share count:** further issuance would dilute the path to $0.97 EPS.
