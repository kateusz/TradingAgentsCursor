## Executive Summary

Take-Two Interactive is a large-cap ($44.9B market cap) video game publisher best known for *Grand Theft Auto*, *NBA 2K*, *Red Dead Redemption*, and mobile titles via Zynga. Fundamentals present a **transitional profile**: trailing GAAP metrics remain distorted by a massive FY2025 impairment, while normalized operating performance and cash generation are materially stronger than headline earnings suggest.

**Key takeaways for traders:**

1. **Earnings quality gap** — TTM EPS is **-$1.59** and net income **-$298M**, but TTM free cash flow is **~$1.45B** and normalized quarterly EBITDA reached **$441M** in the most recent quarter. Amortization of acquisition intangibles (~$303M/quarter) and prior impairments dominate GAAP losses.
2. **Balance sheet repair underway** — Net debt fell from **$2.21B** (Mar 2025) to **$973M** (Mar 2026) via **$1.15B** of debt repayments over four quarters. Liquidity is adequate (current ratio 1.24, **$1.55B** cash).
3. **Revenue stability with margin pressure** — Quarterly revenue has held in a **$1.50B–$1.77B** band over the past year; gross margins remain healthy (~56%) but operating margins are thin or negative due to high R&D and SG&A.
4. **Valuation embeds growth** — Forward P/E of **23.96** on forward EPS of **$10.03** implies significant earnings recovery (likely tied to *GTA VI* cycle). PEG of **3.39** and P/B of **12.69** signal premium pricing relative to book (tangible book is deeply negative).
5. **Insider selling elevated** — President Karl Slatoff sold **~249K shares (~$56.3M)** in early June 2026; CEO Strauss Zelnick sold **~70K shares (~$15.5M)** in late May 2026. Pattern is consistent with scheduled 10b5-1 sales but adds near-term sentiment risk.
