## Price History & Structural Context

### Long-term arc (Aug 2025 – Aug 2026)

Over the 251-session lookback, GENI.US has traced a classic **decline–base–recovery** pattern:

1. **Distribution phase (Aug–Oct 2025):** Price peaked near **$13.55** (Sep 4) and rolled over through Q4 2025, breaking below $11 by October and accelerating to **$8.70** by Nov 20 on rising volume (13.9M shares).

2. **Failed recovery (Dec 2025 – Jan 2026):** A brief rally to **$11.37** (Dec 30) failed to hold; the stock resumed its slide.

3. **Capitulation & base (Feb–Apr 2026):** A catastrophic gap down on **Feb 5** ($8.51 open → $6.19 close on 38.2M volume) marked a regime change. Price ground lower to the cycle low of **$3.87** (Apr 10), then consolidated in the **$4.00–$5.50** range through May.

4. **Recovery leg (Jun–Aug 2026):** A gradual climb from ~$5.40 (Jun 25) accelerated in late July. The Jul 27 session (+12.5% to $7.27 on 12.0M volume) was the first major thrust; Aug 4 extended the move to **$8.08** on the highest volume since the Feb crash.

**Net change over 12 months:** From $12.22 (Aug 5, 2025) to $8.08 — still **−33.9%** year-over-year, underscoring that the current rally is a recovery within a damaged chart, not a confirmed new bull market.
