## Actionable Insights

### For Bullish Traders

1. **Trend is strongly up** — MACD, price above all MAs, and volume-confirmed breakout support continued long exposure.
2. **Pullback entries preferred** — Chasing at $493 with RSI 79 and price above upper Bollinger Band offers poor risk/reward. Wait for retest of $464–475 (S1–S2) or 10 EMA (~$438) for better entries.
3. **Stop placement** — Use ATR-based stops: 1.5× ATR ($26) below entry, or below the July 31 close ($464.72) for swing positions.
4. **Breakout confirmation** — A daily close above $500 on volume >50M would signal continuation toward the $520–530 supply zone.

### For Bearish / Mean-Reversion Traders

1. **Overbought extremes** — RSI 79 + price above upper Bollinger Band = elevated pullback probability.
2. **Gap fill risk** — The July 30 gap (from ~$390 to $451) leaves air pockets below; a fill to $451 would be a −8.5% correction from current levels.
3. **Short-term fade** — Counter-trend shorts targeting $464–475 (S1–S2) with tight stops above $500 may offer favorable risk/reward, but fighting a volume-backed breakout is inherently risky.
4. **200 SMA test** — A pullback to the 200 SMA ($432) would be a healthy retest of the breakout; failure to hold would invalidate the bull case.

### For All Traders

- **Volume matters** — The July 30 (110M) and August 3 (67M) volume spikes confirm institutional participation. Declining volume on further upside would be a warning sign.
- **Event risk** — The July 30 move strongly resembles an earnings or guidance surprise. Monitor for follow-up catalysts or profit-taking by event-driven buyers.
- **Position sizing** — With ATR at $17 (3.4% of price), reduce position size vs. the pre-breakout environment where ATR was ~$12 (3.1% of price at $390).
