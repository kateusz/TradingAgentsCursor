## Executive Summary

G.US has undergone a severe structural downtrend over the past year, falling from the mid-$40s (August 2025) to a cycle low near **$26.85** (June 30, 2026)—a drawdown of roughly **36%** from the December 2025 peak of $47.98. A sharp counter-trend rally began in late July 2026, lifting the stock **~33%** from the June trough to a recent high of **$36.71** (July 29) on elevated volume. As of the last close (**$35.79** on August 4), price sits above all short- and medium-term moving averages but remains **~5.4% below the 200-day SMA ($37.84)**, keeping the long-term trend classified as bearish.

The current setup is best described as a **high-momentum recovery rally inside a broader downtrend**—not yet a confirmed trend reversal. MACD is firmly bullish, RSI is elevated but cooling from overbought extremes, and Bollinger Bands have expanded upward, signaling a volatility breakout. Traders should treat the $36–37 zone (upper Bollinger band and 200 SMA confluence) as the critical resistance band that must be reclaimed for a structural bull case.

## Price Action & Trend Structure

### Phase 1: Gradual Decline (Aug 2025 – Jan 2026)
The stock traded in a $40–48 range through late 2025, peaking at **$47.98** on December 22, 2025. A slow bleed began in January 2026, with closes slipping from $46.90 (Jan 7) to $43.63 (Jan 30).

### Phase 2: Accelerated Selloff (Feb – Jun 2026)
- **February 3, 2026:** A gap-down session from $43.66 to **$38.56** on **4.06M shares** (nearly 2× average volume) marked the first capitulation event.
- **February 11–12:** Further collapse to **$36.78** and **$35.92** on 5.16M and 3.92M volume respectively.
- **April–May 2026:** Price broke below $35, reaching **$33.72** (April 10) and ultimately **$28.77** (May 14) on 4.75M volume.
- **June 18, 2026:** Another high-volume flush to **$28.25** (5.73M shares), establishing the cycle low zone around **$26.85–$28.25**.

### Phase 3: Recovery Rally (Jul – Aug 2026)
From the June 30 close of **$27.50**, price base-built in the $28–30 range through mid-July, then launched:
- **July 28:** +7.5% to **$34.61** on 2.67M volume
- **July 29:** +6.1% to **$36.71** on **3.55M volume** (highest volume in the rally)
- **July 30–31:** Profit-taking to **$35.33** and **$35.17**
- **August 3–4:** Consolidation at **$35.16** and **$35.79**

The rally has recovered approximately **one-third of the total decline** from the December peak, but price remains well below the 200 SMA and the prior support-turned-resistance zone near $38–40.
